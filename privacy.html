Kuricak Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Kuricak is a writing application for macOS designed to provide a simple and customizable typing experience.

Personal Data

Kuricak does not require users to create an account. Documents and text created or opened in Kuricak are processed locally on the user's Mac.

Kuricak does not upload the contents of your documents to a developer-operated server.

Files and Documents

Kuricak accesses files only when the user chooses to open, create, or save them. File access is controlled by macOS and the application's sandbox permissions.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions offered through Kuricak are processed using Apple's App Store and StoreKit services. Payment information is handled by Apple and is not directly collected or stored by Kuricak.

Third-Party Services

Kuricak does not use advertising services or sell personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when Kuricak's features or privacy practices change. Any changes will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, please visit the Kuricak Support page.
